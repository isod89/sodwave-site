No warranty

Installing a custom firmware is done at your own risk. SLOOP is provided as is, without any warranty. Keep a backup of your work, and remember that the official firmware can be put back at any time from the installer page.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and collects no personal data.

  • To show the latest SLOOP version, your browser asks GitHub's public API once an hour (GitHub's privacy statement). The answer is kept in your browser's local storage, nowhere else.
  • The installer and the web editor are served by GitHub Pages. They talk to your FM-1 over USB from your own browser; nothing about your device or your music is sent anywhere.
  • Like any web host, Hostinger may keep technical server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation.
